Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Processing fee: Every refund or cancellation will have a 3% processing fee deducted, calculated on the original amount paid. This fee is non-refundable.

1. Cancellation by the Client

You may cancel an order or engagement at any time by emailing us at saheli.jasmine.infotech@gmail.com with your name, invoice or payment reference, and the reason for cancellation.

  • Before work has started: you will receive a refund of the amount paid, less the 3% processing fee.
  • After work has started: the cost of work already completed (based on milestones delivered or hours spent) will be deducted. The remaining balance will be refunded, less the 3% processing fee.
  • After a milestone or project is delivered and accepted: payments for that milestone or project are not refundable.

2. Cancellation by Jasmine Infotech

If we are unable to start or continue your project for reasons on our side, we will refund the amount paid for work not yet delivered, less the 3% processing fee.

3. Refund Calculation

The 3% processing fee is calculated on the original amount paid, in the same currency you paid in. For example, if you paid ₹10,000 and are eligible for a full refund, the refund amount will be ₹10,000 − ₹300 (3%) = ₹9,700. Similarly, a full refund of a $1,000 payment will be $1,000 − $30 (3%) = $970.

4. Refund Process & Timeline

  • Refund requests are reviewed and confirmed by email within 3–5 business days.
  • Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7–10 business days of approval.
  • The time taken for the amount to appear in your account depends on your bank or card provider.

5. Non-Refundable Items

  • The processing fee (3% of the amount paid).
  • Work that has already been completed, delivered or approved.
  • Third-party costs paid on your behalf, such as domain names, hosting, cloud services or software licences.

6. Contact Us

For any questions about refunds or cancellations, please email saheli.jasmine.infotech@gmail.com. Please also see our Terms & Conditions.
