FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about outsourcing your software work to us.

What services does Jasmine Infotech provide?

We offer custom software development, software testing and QA, software architecture consulting, cloud services, DevOps and CI/CD, and ongoing maintenance and support.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We are based in Hyderabad, India, and work with clients around the world. We plan meetings around overlapping working hours so communication stays smooth.

How do you keep our code and data safe?

We sign NDAs before discussing project details, follow secure coding practices, use access-controlled repositories, and transfer full intellectual property rights to you.

Can you take over an existing project?

Absolutely. We start with a code and architecture review, document what we find, and then continue development, testing or maintenance from where your previous team left off.

How do we get started?

Just send us an email with a short description of your project. We will review it, reply with any questions, and share a proposal with scope, timeline and cost.
